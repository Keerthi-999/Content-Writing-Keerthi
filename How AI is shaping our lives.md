HOW IS AI SHAPING OUR LIVES 


BY: KEERTHI ARUNACHALAM 


AI is driving our future ahead and is constantly defining our thoughts and 
choices. What began as a tool for freeing humans from repetitive and routine tasks is 
now entering the next phase: can produce creative music, content, images and even 
write code.  


Whether it is a personalized ad recommendations on your phone or suggesting your 
next purchase, AI has silently been incorporated in our daily routines. It has made 
lives easier, smoother, fast and more connected than before. As the capabilities of AI 
increase, it is being used to solve various real-world problems. Governments are using 
AI to identify and scale their infrastructure. A recent example is of Bangalore metro 
operations using AI in signaling and train control systems. According to them, AI can 
help operators determine optimum train speeds, regulate services and respond to 
changing operating conditions using large volumes of real-time data. It can also 
anticipate passenger demand and recommend energy efficiency rates and additional 
deployment of trains during peak hours. 


Recent data shows that AI software significantly boosts corporate productivity in 
manufacturing and logistics. Even within software companies, AI is restructuring their 
business models and increasing their overall productivity. However, displacing 
repetitive jobs has created a way for new job opportunities. 



As we talk about sustainable development , AI has become a pivotal tool for 
scientists to predict weather patterns, disasters to prepare before they strike. Even as 
we go further ahead AI is being used in scientific research of identifying  
viruses, molecules aiding in development of human health. Healthcare is also seeing a 
major transformation with AI assist to doctors during operations and critical 
care, and even in diagnosing diseases underlying their ability to simplify things. 


More than a tool , it has rewired our human experience to think beyond 
horizons, leaving us to use our critical thinking, making judgments and plan for the 
future. While it may just be perfect, we may need boundaries that protect our data 
privacy so that AI can aid everyone for a bright, fair and productive future.
