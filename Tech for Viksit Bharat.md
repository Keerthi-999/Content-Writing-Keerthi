Essay Theme: Tech for Viksit Bharat 


From Congestion to Innovation: Redefining Indian cities 


BY: KEERTHI ARUNACHALAM


As India closely marches towards the vision of a "Viksit Bharat" by 2047, the integration of advanced technologies 
into urban planning emerges as a transformative imperative. In this era , defined by a supersonic technological 
evolution, the rollout of 5G technology and the broader technology revolution are instrumental in influencing changes 
in society,drive economic prosperity and enhance global leadership.Coming to India, with a broader,rich and diverse 
landscape, can take a lead to position itself as a global leader,push for robust economic growth & address its widely 
criticized social challenges with a potential harvest from these disruptive technologies. 


Considering here urban chaos as a social challenge , Bangalore is facing certain 
challenges: an unplanned city structure, thereby calling for an innovative and sustainable urban planning 
solution.Bangalore’s traffic congestion highlights the urgent need for a comprehensive and futuristic urban 
planning.The city’s unstructured chaos of growth and infrastructure has been the cause to gridlocked 
thoroughfares.While considering it practically impossible for an existing city to change its structure , coming up here 
is solution for a new city in the future built using this urban planner technology. 


A solution for this calls for cutting - edge technologies and innovation integration for designing a city covering the 
aspects of livability,connectivity,economic vitality,inclusivity,efficiency and sustainability.It ensures that city’s vivid 
resources are optimally used ensuring a well-life of residents. We need real-time data to analyse land usage,resource 
distribution & also understand population density and dynamics in order to create the plan so that it can be used to 
design appropriate & efficient layouts,optimize land use and cover all facilities that a city should provide. 
Going back in history , we should appreciate Soviet Union who have implemented an organized strategy to urban city 
structures.These cities were built mainly focusing with a highly centralized urban planning,utility infrastructure, 
extensive public transport networks,green spaces and public amenities and well-defined residential , economic and 
industrial zones.This could be a source of inspiration for Indian cities to reform for a more sustainable future.By 
integrating advanced technologies,new forming cities can avoid the pitfalls of rigid planning while ensuring well all 
around development. 


Combining various mathematical and analytical approaches with 5G technology can create a comprehensive solution 
for urban planning in a new city. Integration of 5G and IoT(Internet of Things) can be a revolutionizing tool for urban 
planning.Installing IoT sensors can help to collect real-time data of traffic flow,air quality & energy consumption.This 
can later be transmitted via 5G networks to help in the decision making process.For instance, in transportation , a 
means of ensuring connectivity , residents should be encouraged to use public transport rather than private vehicles 
to reduce carbon footprint.While it’s difficult to rely on accessibility of buses in remote areas , 5G can a become a 
connect to residents and the network of buses by providing services like real-time tracking and route optimizations 
enhancing efficiency and reliability.Using differential equations and dynamic systems , a simulation can be created to 
predict future of new infrastructure projects,population growth, and many other factors.Advanced optimization 
techniques can be used for dynamic traffic management & energy distribution.These techniques also ensure the 
functionality of public facilities and accessibility to all.Statistical analysis,machine learning and game theory can 
envision a wide range of urban phenomena,such as public health,traffic and more.All these predictions will be useful 
to mitigate potential issues and enhance the quality of urban life. 


This unified solution framework platform should provide an interface , i.e. an app which encourages public 
engagement from all classes of society to be a part of the process for a sustainable city.It should provide real-time insights 
on urban services, and urge people feedback for guaranteeing a good governance.Cities can achieve a harmonious 
balance between growth and livability by implementing a framework that integrates 5G technologies and advanced 
analytical approaches. This comprehensive framework encompasses various aspects of urban planning, including 
transportation, housing, public services, environmental management, and economic development.Ensuring green 
spaces, sustainable building practices, and environmental management can mitigate the environmental impact of 
urbanization. Utilizing technologies like Augmented Reality a lows developers to visualize and refine urban designs 
before implementation, ensuring that sustainability is integrated into every aspect of city development and gives 
people an opportunity to pour in their voices for the growth of a unified and prosperous city. 



By learning from traffic congestion and urban congestion challenges from existing cities like Bangalore , a new city can 
be designed prioritizing sustainability,efficiency and livability with this platform.Building a new city is an arena for 
integrating advanced technologies and innovations from the ground up.Incorporating the latest technologies such as 
5G,Iot(Internet of Things),Augmented Reality in this planning platform ensures a well-organized and resilient urban 
environment. As India strides towards its vision of a "Viksit Bharat" by 2047, the creation of new, technologically 
advanced cities will be crucial in achieving the goal of a developed and prosperous nation, also ensuring the position 
of India as a global leader in urban development and in this digital age. When these technologies shape India 
economy, there will be an exponential economic growth attracting foreign players to enter, fostering innovation and 
also creating employment for its citizens.This integration is a small step towards a big goal.
Enabling this platform as a solution for the masses will significantly enhance 
accessibility and provide widespread benefits. Thereby, the government should build a thriving technological 
ecosystem,so as to achieve the goal of a ‘Viksit Bharat’ 2047.
