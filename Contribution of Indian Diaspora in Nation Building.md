Contribution of Indian Diaspora in Nation Building 


BY:KEERTHI ARUNACHALAM


A mere nation’s development is not only concerned with its homogenous population , it has 
been constantly influenced by the Indians living overseas.While we see the progress 
towards a Viksit Bharat 2047 , the flow of Indian students and immigrants to other 
countries has doubled up.  


The endless flow of Indian immigrants  setting in and settled  in the US ,UK, 
Australia,Germany and beyond , for have been endless stories of giving up everything in 
India to enter these developed nations to have a better life ahead.There have been a lot of 
success stories where they turn to become the highest earning,have well-paying jobs,or even positioned 
in government jobs, while some end up to live in a state of despair. 


While in the optimistic side,where Indians have lived through the race and have got 
experience of rich tapestry of life are helping India to grow indirectly.How they contribute in 
ways of depositing their high valued currency into their Indian Overseas & NRI accounts in 
Indian banks they indirectly push our economy in bright light ensuring a sudden surplus 
cash flow to the economy which can be used in infrastructure development such in 
construction of bridges,railway,roadway networks and even in ensuring air connectivity 
ensuring Indians try to appreciate their home with once in a year visit and trying to be a 
part of the development process as the PM said in a Dubai Indian Summit 
late last year.The already settled in Indian Diaspora who are from older generations 
ensured their stability in India by buying plots of acres of land just in case of any emergency 
or back-off,which in a manner has lead to appreciation in land rates for ever-booming 
real-estate sector. 


India has produced endless talents who have gone onto to bring glory to this nation.In 
current Indian and Indian-origin students who are strong in AI research are dominating this 
field,there unknown talents from the nation working for the best companies in the world 
for any industry you can imagine.Not only AI,5G,technology,medical and biotech 
research,industries and more there is a adept growth of Indian researchers,pioneers,highly 
valued professionals being created.There are many people who came through all
hurdles,passing every test of life and reaching success like the example of Sundar 
Pichai,Satya Nadella and many more unknown names in various fields.They have brought 
fame name that Indians need to keep up by upskilling themselves and be constantly competitive 
with the world.Many have gone from Indian education to such ambitious goals in life.This is 
something 90% positive,10% negative is that it is causing a talent drain , while they learn 
and give back to their world, it reaches hardly their home especially in our nation.But 
thanks to our initiatives by Prime Minister,by connecting with other 
nations either trade,security or diplomatic-wise , India is on momentum to bring home 
those experiences of the talented who left India by constant interactions with those 
professionals to be back on home soil,ensuring India is on a path to development.

In conclusion once India diaspora start appreciating back their home country in every form from culture to finance , India will 
see and set its path to a bright future with ambitious goal of being a developed nation on 
its 100th year of Independence(2047) encompassing the growth of gdp & economic 
growth,development,social process,environmental sustainability and good governance.
